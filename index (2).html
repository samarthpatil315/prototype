About the setting

A peaceful, farm-surrounded place to eat

Jungle Restaurant is a family-friendly rural dining spot in Ambewadi, near Prayag Chikhali. Its peaceful, breezy, farm-surrounded setting is the backdrop for a local Kolhapuri food identity.

The menu includes local Kolhapuri specialties alongside chicken dishes, kebabs and egg curry. Guest feedback on the food is mixed; the ambience is the strongest positive. Some guests mention slow service and longer waits at peak hours.
